# E-Commerce-Sales-Analysis

#Overall Sales: The total sales were approximately ₹21.75 lakh from 2000 orders.  
     -This helped me understand the overall sales performance of the business. 

 
#Category-wise Sales: The Sports category had the highest sales and then Electronics has the next highest sales. Beauty had the lowest sales among the five categories.  
     -This shows that customers purchased more products from the Sports category. 

 
#Region-wise Sales: The North region recorded the highest sales, while the West region had the lowest sales. 

 
#Payment Methods: PayPal recorded the highest sales among the three payment methods followed by Credit Card and Cash on Delivery (COD). 
     -The difference between the payment methods was not very large. 

 
#Average Order Value: The average sales amount per order was around ₹1087.  
     -This gave me an idea of how much customers spent on an average order. 

 
#Monthly Sales: The monthly sales summary helped me compare sales across different months and identify periods with higher and lower sales. 

##Conclusion: 
   This project helped me learn how Excel can be used to clean data, apply formulas, summarise information and create charts and dashboards. I understood how sales data can be analysed category-wise, region-wise and payment-wise. Based on the findings, the business can focus on improving sales in the lower-performing region and continue monitoring the categories that contribute more to overall sales.
